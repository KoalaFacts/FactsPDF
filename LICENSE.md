## 3. Revenue, control, and reassessment

"Group" means You and all entities that directly or indirectly control You,
are controlled by You, or are under common control with You. Control means
more than 50 percent of voting rights or an enforceable ability to direct an
entity's management. A common minority investor without control does not by
itself combine otherwise independent organizations.

"Annual Gross Revenue" means Group-wide revenue from all external operations,
not merely revenue from a particular product or from PDF generation, before
operating costs, wages, other expenses, and income taxes. Use the Group's
ordinary consistently applied financial accounting policies. Exclude genuine
sales returns, sales taxes collected for a government, intercompany revenue
counted elsewhere in the consolidation, equity investment, and loan principal.
Include trading, licensing, subscription, service, and other operating revenue.
This is not profit, valuation, cash balance, or funding raised.

The measurement period is the Group's most recently completed full 12-month
financial year. If no such year exists, use the preceding 12 months, or all
actual operating history if shorter, measured at the end of each calendar
month. Do not annualize a partial operating history. Artificial changes to
financial year dates do not restart eligibility; during a resulting gap use
the preceding 12 months. A predecessor's operations are included when a
business is merely transferred to a new entity.

Convert non-USD revenue using published period-average exchange rates from a
central bank or an established financial institution for the same measurement
period. Apply one documented method consistently, aggregate the Group before
comparing with the threshold, and do not round a figure above the threshold
down to obtain eligibility.

Reassess at the beginning of each financial year and whenever control changes;
entities without a full financial year reassess monthly. After a control
change, include the new Group using a common measurement period and eliminate
intragroup duplication. Deferment of accounts, a change of account, outsourcing,
a shell entity, or division of one controlled business does not change the
underlying assessment.

Keep reasonable internal records supporting the category claimed. No financial
submission or registration is required for ordinary Community use. A reasonable
written request describing a specific eligibility concern may seek a signed
eligibility confirmation and proportionate supporting information. This is not
permission for unrestricted audits, access to systems, or publication of
confidential financial records. Agree on a secure confidential channel before
sharing sensitive information; do not post it in public issues.

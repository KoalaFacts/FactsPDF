## Community License 1.1 - 9 October 2026

The current controlling text is [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md). Version 1.1 adds
mandatory public disclosure of Covered Modifications to the engine and its
corresponding source/build materials, including internal operational use and
hosted services. It defines a controlled development/testing exception, public
access and source-retention requirements, independent-application exclusions,
and the standard Commercial License's source-publication policy.

The revenue threshold and alternative charity/open-source-project eligibility
categories are not changed. No paid contract or legal-review completion is
created by publishing these documents.

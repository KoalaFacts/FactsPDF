## Follow-up independent review: continuation padding and border clipping

9. The independent review of `8797ea5e` reported two **P2** issues: [consumed top padding repeated on continuation pages](https://github.com/KoalaFacts/FactsPDF/pull/14#discussion_r4237785144) and [a border extending beyond a short first/last fragment](https://github.com/KoalaFacts/FactsPDF/pull/14#discussion_r4237785145). Tests in `d379070359fd663b1a6ac1c5f514c8331095d66f` reproduced both. [RED CI 38056342364](https://github.com/KoalaFacts/FactsPDF/actions/runs/38056342364) reported **452 passed / 2 failed**: the next-page baseline was 20pt too low and a 5pt-high fragment emitted a 10pt-high border.
10. Commits `fa66e5163abdf4005f064060719445773c316605` and `e44277ffb1be9f3fd61541a4a2b241f961a03a73` share clipped top/bottom border heights between `PaintedBox.CommandCount` and the ASCII/Unicode painter. This keeps short fragment rectangles within bounds and zero-height fragments at zero commands.
11. `561f9c70d62df62d19a9d44b2034102c1cc5d9b7` adds mixed ancestor/new-child padding, empty-final-page closure and zero-height-border regressions. [RED CI 38056749562](https://github.com/KoalaFacts/FactsPDF/actions/runs/38056749562) had **456 passed / 4 failed**; the clipping regression and three zero-height variants passed, while the original and three expanded padding cases failed.
12. `3f93ca36ad6f326e901733921caf375cbb8f9f22` tracks consumed leading space per open scope, recomputes the pending leading total at page transitions and prevents reintroducing consumed top padding when closing a textless continuation. New children keep their own unconsumed padding. Purely unpainted scopes outside a continued painted ancestor retain the pre-existing M6 first-text behavior. [GREEN CI 38057099068](https://github.com/KoalaFacts/FactsPDF/actions/runs/38057099068) reports **460/460 NUnit** on Ubuntu; the Windows/macOS test jobs also passed. Verify the final documentation head's AOT, visual and independent review checks before merge.
